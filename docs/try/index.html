Compare a declared reference with an observed PDF conversion. The real BioSURE Python text workflow and trained paragraph-correspondence model run inside your browser. They identify review locations and may propose wording copied from your declared reference; they do not authenticate that source or approve an edit.

Your PDF and paragraph text are processed in browser memory, not uploaded to BioSURE or a model service. Version-pinned Pyodide and PDF.js code may download from jsDelivr; the CDN sees ordinary connection metadata, not your document bytes. Do not use patient or confidential data in a competition demo.

1. Supply two versions

Paste paragraph text separated by blank lines. For a PDF, import its text layer into one side, then compare every extracted page with the original and correct reading order, spacing and paragraph boundaries yourself. Scanned PDFs need OCR or manual transcription outside this page.

PDF extraction yields unverified page-text chunks, not paragraphs. It can omit images, tables, equations, or rearrange columns. A file hash or your acknowledgement is not proof that the reference is genuine.
